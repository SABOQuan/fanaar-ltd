Purpose

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Fanaar uses on our website, why we use them, and how you can control them.

Scope

Applies to www.fanaarltd.com only. It does not apply to cookies or tracking technology used on a client's own website, which is addressed separately under that client's engagement with Fanaar.

Policy Statement

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it.

Categories we use

CategoryPurposeCan be disabled?
Strictly necessaryEnable core Site functionality, such as navigation and secure form submissionNo — required for the Site to work
FunctionalRemember preferences such as cookie consent choicesYes
AnalyticsHelp us understand aggregate visitor behaviour (for example, via Google Analytics) so we can improve the SiteYes
Marketing / advertisingMeasure the effectiveness of our own marketing (for example, Meta Pixel or LinkedIn Insight Tag) and, where relevant, support retargetingYes

Managing your preferences

Where non-essential cookies are used, we seek your consent through a cookie banner on first visit. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this Site and revisiting, or through your browser's cookie settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies; instructions vary by browser and are available from your browser's help documentation.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use (such as analytics or advertising platforms). These providers have their own privacy and cookie practices, which we encourage you to review on their respective sites.

Your Responsibilities

  • Set your cookie preferences through our consent banner or your browser settings according to your comfort level.
  • Review third-party opt-out tools if you wish to limit tracking across multiple sites.

Fanaar's Responsibilities

  • Only deploy non-essential cookies with your consent.
  • Keep this list of cookie categories reasonably current as our tooling changes.

Contact Information

Questions about this policy can be sent to our team at fanaarlimited@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge every policy-related enquiry within three (3) business days.
